Meet Kasa

Five directions for a mascot. Each one is a little pile of something, drawn with the same ink line, blush, and palette as the product.

Pino

A little stack of paper with a push pin for a hat. The most literal Kasa: it is the pile.

Best for app icon, empty states, onboarding

Kivi

A calm stone cairn with a lingonberry sprig. Finnish forest energy: steady, patient, always there.

Best for brand story, merch, calmer tone

Lappu

A sticky note that waves hello. Chatty and bright, the friend who always has an idea.

Best for social, stickers, reactions

Ruska

A cosy pile of autumn leaves, named after the Finnish word for autumn colours. Warm and a bit mischievous.

Best for seasonal moments, playful campaigns

Kasa Bot

The bot's own character: a pine index card with a pin antenna, always carrying something it found for you.

Best for the bot's avatar, loading and thinking states
