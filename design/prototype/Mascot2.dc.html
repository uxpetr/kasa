Meet Kasa, round 2

One soft, white, abstract creature with the same friendly face, in four silhouettes. No outlines, just smooth shading, so it glows on pine green. A lingonberry is its one signature detail.

Heap

A soft little mound, the shape of a pile. Wide and settled at the bottom, so it looks calm and hard to knock over.

Pebble

A smooth, slightly tilted egg, like a river stone you'd pick up. The berry sits off to the side like a hair clip.

Drop

A soft drop with a little curl on top. The most expressive silhouette; the curl can bounce when it talks.

Stack

Two soft stones stacked: a pile of one on top of one. Reads as Kasa even as a tiny icon.
